Public Safety - Emergency Management

Phone: (828) 349-2067

The Macon County Emergency Response Plan, MSDS, and Inventory Forms received by the Local Emergency Planning Committee are available for public review. Contact the Emergency Management office for details.

Macon County Emergency Management continuously strives to improve the county's emergency services and disaster preparedness. Residents are encouraged to prepare individually and with their families.

Services Provided

  • 9-1-1 Communications
  • 9-1-1 Addressing
  • Emergency Management
  • Emergency Medical Services
  • Fire Marshal's Office

Our department oversees contracts with 11 volunteer fire departments, manages fire inspections, and enforces the county's fire prevention ordinance. We coordinate emergency and non-emergency support between local, state, and federal agencies during disasters.
